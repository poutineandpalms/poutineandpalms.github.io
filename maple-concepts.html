What the research changed: I started with four variations of a gold spinning ring (my default pattern). Reading about Gemini's new UI — "smooth, shifting gradients" with a "heartbeat" pulse — pushed me toward flowing color instead of spinning geometry (Concept A). The Assistant "light bar" pattern gave me Concept D, which I wouldn't have drawn otherwise. Nest Hub's "ambient, blends into the room" philosophy killed my first instinct for a neon glow and produced the soft diffused halo in Concept C. All four keep your constraints: light theme, avatar left (shrinks when talking), transcript right with more room, no floating, no EQ bars, 🍁 present.
