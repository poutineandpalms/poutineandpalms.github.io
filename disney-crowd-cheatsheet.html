Why this order

  1. Animal Kingdom is structurally the lighter park (4/10 October average vs 6/10 for the Studios), so it absorbs a busy Saturday better.
  2. Mondays run light at Animal Kingdom. TouringPlans recently adjusted their AK Monday estimates down a point because those days have been coming in lighter than their models predicted.
  3. Saturdays skew heavier at Hollywood Studios (weekend locals plus race weekend crowds), while Monday gives you a calmer Studios day.

Good to know

  • Both days rate 8/10 resort-wide, so plan for busy either way: rope drop, Lightning Lane for the headliners, and a midday break.
  • Crowd levels are estimates, not guarantees. Even the best forecasters land within a level or two on any given day.
  • Animal Kingdom closes earlier than the other parks (typically 6 PM), so start there early on Saturday.
